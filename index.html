    Parent PIN

    Parent settings

    Parent PIN Off by default. Hold the clock to open settings. Turn on to also require a four-digit PIN. App restrictions work either way.
    App access
    School only opens straight into School and keeps Home there. Custom lets you choose the available apps. Set a parent PIN to protect restrictions.
    Play time
    When the time is up, the voice says it's almost time for a break and the child can finish what they're doing (up to two minutes); then a sleepy owl covers the toy. Only a grown-up starts more play: hold the clock (and enter the PIN, if set). Only time with the toy on screen counts, and each day starts fresh.
    Incoming calls
    Volume
    Sound when the phone is on silent
    On: the toy plays even with the ring/silent switch set to silent. Off: it follows the switch.
    Camera
    Pretend: drag around a pixel-art safari. Real: the phone's own camera, with fun looks, and animal costumes for selfies. Both: the kids switch between them. Photos stay on this phone.
    Pretend camera
    Move the phone: the pretend scenes go all the way round. Turn around to see the rest, tip the phone up for the sky and down for the ground; dragging still works too. Laid flat, the phone goes back to dragging. Safari asks once to allow motion: tap Move the phone here to answer it. Drag only: the scenes move only when dragged.
    Tilt games
    Ball Trail and Star Flight: tip the phone to steer, or hold a finger where to go. Safari asks once to allow motion: tap Tilt and touch here to answer it (or Start on the getting-ready card), so the question never pops up during play.
    ABC Snack
    The animal asks for a letter out loud and the child finds it (the letter is never shown first). Grows: big letters by name until the child gets eight in a row, then mostly little letters. The animals always ask by the letter's name ("Puppy wants an S!", "Frog wants a little b!"), never its sound. Letters come a few at a time, A to Z like the Letter Path.
    123 Snack
    Count myself: the animal asks for a number; the child adds snacks (tap a box to take one back) and taps the green check when it's right. Too few or too many is said gently, and after two tries the boxes it wants glow. Help count: the boxes show how many, and the animal eats as soon as they're full.
    Letter Path
    One letter at a time, A to Z: meet it, trace it, find the picture that starts with it, then find the letter. The lessons use letter names for now (the sounds are on each letter's page, to check). In order: each letter opens when the one before it is done. All open: any letter, any time. Tap a letter below to hear it and see how to say its sound.
